Linkmo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

IndieH2J (“we” or “us”) operates the Linkmo Android app and the Linkmo web app (https://indieh2j.github.io/app/), together the “Service”. This policy explains what personal information the Service processes, why and how we process it, and the rights you have. It is written to meet Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR and UK GDPR).

Summary

  • We process your account ID and your memos (links, titles, categories, tags and comments) to save them and sync them between your devices.
  • Your own device fetches the original Instagram or YouTube information. Our servers never connect to those services, and we do not store full captions, video descriptions or thumbnails.
  • The Android app shows Google AdMob ads, and you can opt out of personalized ads. The web app has no ads and no usage analytics.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in the app settings.

4. Retention and deletion

How long we keep information

  • Account, memos, settings and ad-free status: until you delete your account.
  • Accounts used without signing in (anonymous accounts not linked to Google): the account and its memos are deleted after more than 12 months without use. When you merge an anonymous account into a Google account, the anonymous account is deleted as soon as its memos have been moved.
  • What remains after account deletion: purchase records for ad removal products (order ID, hash of the purchase token, product, subscription expiry time, refund or cancellation status) are kept with the account ID removed. We need them to handle refunds and the subscription status notifications Google Play sends (renewals, cancellations, refunds), and we keep them only as long as that purpose requires.
  • Inquiry emails: kept as long as needed to handle the inquiry or request, then deleted.
  • Information processed by Google (crash logs, usage analytics, advertising information and so on): see the retention periods in the table in section 5.

How we delete information

  1. When you confirm account deletion in the app, our server (Cloud Functions) deletes all memos, settings and ad-free status of the account, removes the account ID from purchase records and then deletes the sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  2. After the server deletion succeeds, the app also clears scheduled reminders, the share queue, device settings and the offline copy on that device.
  3. If you ask by email, we verify that the request is yours and delete the account the same way.
  4. Anonymous accounts are deleted automatically by a server job that regularly checks when each account was last used.
  5. Deleted data is removed from the database and is not restored. In the backup systems of our service provider (Google), it is removed on Google’s deletion schedule (for example, within 180 days for Firebase Authentication data).

6. How original information is fetched

To organize a link automatically, your device (the Android app or your web browser) requests information about that one post directly from the original service.

  • YouTube: the video title and channel name are requested from YouTube’s public oEmbed address on youtube.com (Android app and web app).
  • Instagram: only in the Android app, the public page of that one post is requested from instagram.com to read its caption and author. Instagram links saved in the web app are organized on your phone the next time you open the Android app linked to the same Google account, or when a link you share on that phone starts background organizing. Links you share are also requested this way in the background, without opening the app.
  • Your device’s IP address is sent to that service (Google for YouTube, Meta for Instagram), and its privacy policy applies. These requests carry no sign-in information or cookies.
  • Our servers never connect to Instagram or YouTube.
  • We store only the memo created from the information (title, category, tags, author display name), never the full caption, video description or thumbnail. Videos, audio and images are never downloaded.

7. Files the web app loads from Google servers

When you open the web app, your browser downloads files it needs from Google servers:

  • accounts.google.com: the Google sign-in script
  • www.gstatic.com: the Flutter engine that runs the web app, and the Firebase web libraries
  • fonts.gstatic.com: fonts

Connection information such as your IP address may be sent to Google when this happens, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

8. Behavioral information and personalized ads

The Android app shows Google AdMob ads in the memo list, and you can watch a rewarded ad from the settings when you choose to. The web app has no ads.

  • Items collected: advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (approximate location), ad request and interaction information
  • How: the AdMob SDK in the app collects them automatically when it loads ads and sends them to Google.
  • Purposes: showing and measuring ads, ads based on your interests (personalized ads), preventing fraud such as invalid clicks
  • Retention: per Google’s retention policies (see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services).
  • How to opt out
    • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings app. The menu differs between devices; it is usually under “Google → Ads” or “Privacy → Ads”.
    • Where a consent screen applies, such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states, you can change your choices at any time under [Privacy options] in the app settings.
    • Where there is no consent screen, such as Korea, use the advertising ID settings above to opt out of personalized ads.
  • Consent to personalized ads is separate from the consent required to use the Service. If you refuse, every feature still works and you see non-personalized ads.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, if you say you are under 16, we do not use personalized ads and we apply age-restricted ad treatment.
  • No ads are loaded while you are ad-free (24 hours from a rewarded ad, the monthly subscription or the lifetime purchase).

9. Automatic collection and how to refuse it

  • Web app: we do not use cookies for advertising or analytics. Your sign-in state, an offline copy of your memos and settings such as “detect copied links” are kept in browser storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes them; you will then need to sign in again, and changes that have not synced yet may be lost. In the Google sign-in window, Google may use its own cookies under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Android app: instead of cookies, the Google SDKs in the app use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, app set ID and Firebase installation ID. You can opt out of personalized ads as described in section 8. Crash log collection cannot be turned off separately in the app; usage analytics can be refused on the consent screen or under [Privacy options] in the app settings in the EEA, UK and Switzerland.
  • These information pages: no cookies and no scripts.

10. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, and withdraw consent. Users in the EEA and UK can also ask us to restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and receive a copy of their data (data portability).

  • In the app: edit or delete memos, delete your account in the settings, and change ad and usage analytics consent under [Privacy options] (where available).
  • By email: send requests for access, a copy of your data or anything else to indieh2j@gmail.com. To help us verify you, send it from the email address of the Google account linked to Linkmo. Accounts used without signing in (anonymous accounts) are not tied to an email address, so please delete memos or the account directly in the app.
  • Deadlines: for users in Korea, we act within 10 days of receiving the request and tell you the reason if we extend the deadline or refuse. For users in the EEA and UK, we respond within one month.
  • Users in the EEA and UK can complain to the data protection authority in their country (section 13).

11. Age requirement (14 and older)

  • You must be 14 or older to use the Service. The Android app asks on first launch and the web app asks after your first sign-in, and no memos are saved before you confirm.
  • We do not accept personal information from children under 14. If you learn that a child under 14 has used the Service, tell us at indieh2j@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, an age of 16 applies to consent for personalized ads, so the app also asks once whether you are 16 or older and keeps the answer only on your device. If you are under 16, we do not use personalized ads, we apply age-restricted ad treatment, and we do not collect usage analytics.

12. Security measures

  • All communication between the apps and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Database security rules let only you access your own data. Purchase records and ad-free status can be written only by our server.
  • We store only what we need: no full captions, descriptions or thumbnails from the original posts and no reasons for failed organizing; no links, titles or captions in usage analytics or logs; and tracking parameters are removed from links.
  • Purchase tokens are kept only as hashes, never in their original form.
  • Access from the Android app is monitored with app integrity checks (Firebase App Check).
  • Secrets used to run the Service, such as signing keys and service account keys, are kept separate from the source code.
